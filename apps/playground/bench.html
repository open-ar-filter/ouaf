Ouaf benchmark

Renders each case frame by frame on its test clip, as fast as this device can (device adaptation off), and times each frame from tracking to the GPU finishing it. The first frames of each case are a warm-up, not counted. ?case=<name> (repeatable) picks cases, &filter=<url> (repeatable), &source=<clip> and &maxFaces=N add a stack of your own, &frames=N, &warmup=N, &backend=litert|GPU|CPU, &autorun. Results stay on this device unless you download or copy them.
