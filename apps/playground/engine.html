Ouaf engine

?source=<clip> plays test-media/<clip>.mp4 (camera otherwise), &filter=<url> (repeatable) loads filter folders, e.g. /filters/<id>/, or .ouaf packages, e.g. /filters/<id>.ouaf, &maxFaces=N, &seed=N, &autostart. Device adaptation: &throttle=<ms> simulates slow tracking (also in the panel), &adapt=1|0 turns the governor on or off (default: on for the camera or with a throttle), &signals compares the expression signals from blendshapes and from the geometry, &backend=CPU|GPU forces the tracker backend, &backend=measure measures it again. Open a .ouaf file below, or drop it on the page.
