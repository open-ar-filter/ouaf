Ouaf engine

?source=<clip> plays test-media/<clip>.mp4, ?source=camera-back the rear camera (the front camera otherwise); the picker below changes it, live once started, &filter=<url> (repeatable) loads filter folders, e.g. /filters/<id>/, or .ouaf packages, e.g. /filters/<id>.ouaf, &maxFaces=N, &seed=N, &autostart, &sandbox=cross (scripts in a sandbox from the other loopback name, localhost ↔ 127.0.0.1). With npm run dev, a folder written as filter.ts loads too, and a filter of filters/ reloads when one of its files changes. Device adaptation: &throttle=<ms> simulates slow tracking (also in the panel), &adapt=1|0 turns the governor on or off (default: on for the camera or with a throttle), &signals compares the expression signals from blendshapes and from the geometry, &backend=CPU|GPU forces the tracker backend, &backend=measure measures it again. The stack below adds, orders, turns on and off and removes filters, and keeps them in the URL (&off=<i>: the i-th filter is off); drop a .ouaf file on the page to add it.
